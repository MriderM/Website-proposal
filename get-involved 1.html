Get Involved

The Community Book Nook thrives because of people like you. Here are some ways you can be part of our story.

🤝

Volunteer

Help with story time, shelving books, or running events. Gain experience and meet wonderful people.

💰

Become a Sponsor

Support our programs and help us buy new books. We offer sponsorship packages for businesses and individuals.

📚

Donate Books

We welcome gently used books for all ages. Your donations help keep our shelves full and our readers happy.

🎤

Host a Workshop

Have a skill to share? We'd love to host your workshop, reading, or community event at the Book Nook.
