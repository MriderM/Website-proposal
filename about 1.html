Our Story

A Community Built on Books

The Community Book Nook began as a small pop-up library in a local community centre in 2018. Founded by retired teacher Eleanor Vance, it started with just a few shelves of donated books.

Its popularity grew as it became a gathering place for local book clubs, children's story hours, and community events. In 2021, it moved into a permanent, larger space to accommodate its expanding collection and programs.

Today, the Book Nook is a cornerstone of the community — a vibrant, inclusive hub where everyone is welcome to read, learn, and connect.

👩‍🏫

Meet Our Founder: Eleanor Vance

Eleanor is a retired teacher with over 30 years of experience in education. Her passion for literacy and community led her to create the Book Nook as a space where everyone — regardless of age or background — could access the joy of reading.

"I believe that every book has the power to change a life. The Book Nook is my way of sharing that power with our community."

🎯 Our Mission

To foster a lifelong love of reading and learning by providing free and equitable access to books and educational resources for all members of our community.

🌟 Our Vision

To be a vibrant, inclusive hub where the community connects, learns, and grows through the power of stories.
